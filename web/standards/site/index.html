Abstract

Every page on felipeharker.org and the sites beside it is built on one stylesheet. This page renders each of its tokens and components exactly as the other pages receive them. The reasoning is written down in web/standards/docs in the hark_web repository; this page is for seeing it.

Colour

White, ink, two greys of text and two of surface, and one grey hairline. There is no hue: the accent is ink, so a link is known by its underline or its place on the page. Change --color-accent and its hover and wash follow.

Space

Every margin, padding and gap is one of nine steps. Sections sit --space-8 apart, and the colophon --space-9 below the last of them; rows within a section, --space-5 to --space-6.

Grid

Every page is laid on two columns. The left one holds labels; everything a reader reads starts in the right one, at the middle of the page. A work list, a plate or a map spans both. The two share the width equally until the content column would drop below 560px; below 860px they become one column, each label above what it labels. This page is laid on it, and so is every example below.
